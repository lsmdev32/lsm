# LSM Law Firm Website

The public website of an Indonesian law firm together with the internal dashboard that curates it: attorney profiles, two kinds of editorial content, and the firm's legal-service copy.

## Language

### Attorneys

**Attorney**:
A lawyer of the firm with a public profile, a rank, and optional authored content.
_Avoid_: Staff, lawyer, person, member, employee

**Attorney Rank**:
The attorney's standing in the firm — founder, partner, associate, or of counsel. Decides where the attorney is presented on the public site; it grants no access rights.
_Avoid_: Role, position, level, staff type, title

**Attorney Profile**:
The public page of one attorney: portrait, short bio, full bio, CV, LinkedIn link, and the publications they authored.
_Avoid_: Staff profile, bio page, team member page

**Authorship**:
The optional relationship between an Attorney and the Content they are credited on. Content may have no author.
_Avoid_: Owner, creator, contributor, uploader

### Content

**Content**:
Any editorial item the firm publishes publicly: a Blog or a Publication. Both carry a title, slug, cover image, category, tags, body, author, and publication state.
_Avoid_: Post, article, entry, item, asset

**Blog**:
Content authored for the firm's own site — commentary, insight, firm news.
_Avoid_: News, post, article, insight

**Publication**:
Content representing the firm's formal legal writing, distributed as a downloadable PDF alongside an on-site summary.
_Avoid_: Paper, report, journal article, news

**Publication File**:
The PDF attached to a Publication.
_Avoid_: Attachment, document, PDF, upload

**Publication State**:
Whether a Content item is publicly visible: Draft or Published. Public surfaces show Published content only.
_Avoid_: Status, visibility, live state, active flag

**Draft**:
A Content item that is authored but not publicly visible.
_Avoid_: Not published, unpublished, inactive, pending

**Published**:
A Content item that is publicly visible and reachable by URL.
_Avoid_: Live, released, active, terbit

**Publication Date**:
The date a Content item is dated by; used for display, feed ordering, and year filtering. Independent of Publication State.
_Avoid_: Created date, release date, upload date

**Slug**:
The URL-safe name that identifies a Blog or Publication in public URLs. Unique across all Content.
_Avoid_: Handle, permalink, path, URL name

**Category**:
The single curated topic a Content item belongs to. Every Content item has exactly one.
_Avoid_: Topic, section, practice area, tag

**Tag**:
A free-form label attached to a Content item. Not curated, and not an entity in its own right.
_Avoid_: Label, keyword, topic, category

### Curation

**Pin**:
Marking the one Content item the home page presents as its latest piece, overriding the newest-first feed.
_Avoid_: Feature, highlight, spotlight, stick

**Curated Order**:
An editor-defined sequence for how attorneys are listed in a given public placement — home page, practice area page, or attorney directory.
_Avoid_: Sort order, ranking, position, priority

### Legal Services

**Practice Area**:
A legal-service domain the firm presents publicly, such as arbitration or bankruptcy. Editorial copy about the firm's capabilities, not a Content Category.
_Avoid_: Category, sector, service, expertise

**Specialized Sector**:
The second tier of Practice Areas, framed by client industry — for example energy, education, or elections.
_Avoid_: Industry, niche, secondary practice area

### Public Inquiries

**Contact Inquiry**:
A message a visitor submits through the Contact Us form.
_Avoid_: Message, feedback, ticket

**Job Application**:
A submission through the Careers form from someone seeking employment.
_Avoid_: Application, CV submission, candidate

**Newsletter Subscriber**:
A person who leaves a name and email through the footer to receive firm updates.
_Avoid_: Member, contact, lead
